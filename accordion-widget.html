Account Questions

When logging in, click "Forgot your password?" You should receive a password reset email within a few minutes with instructions to reset your password. For security reasons, we cannot view or retrieve your previous password.

If you don’t receive the reset email, check your spam or junk folder and verify that you're using the correct email associated with your account.

If you still need assistance, contact your district representative or email AEA Learning Online support at support@aealearningonline.org.

You should reach out to your district contact first. If that is not possible, you can submit a support ticket by going support@aealearningonline.org.
