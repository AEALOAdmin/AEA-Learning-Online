Account Questions

If you don’t receive the reset email, check your spam or junk folder and verify that you're using the correct email associated with your account.

If you still need assistance, contact your district representative or email AEA Learning Online support at support@aealearningonline.org.

You should reach out to your district contact first. If that is not possible, you can submit a support ticket by going support@aealearningonline.org.

Training Questions

When you see this message it means your account does not show you as an AEA or school district employee. Only AEA and school district employees are entitled to receive mandatory training at no cost. Follow the directions listed here to update your district of employment.

Please keep in mind that we do not offer refunds. We will not refund participants if they fail to update their district of employment prior to taking mandatory training.

If a training is updated or discontinued, the retake button will not appear under the module title in your training history. If this is the case, try searching the catalog for the training.

Some training modules that commonly will not have a retake button include:
Mandatory Reporter training
Adverse Childhood Experiences: From Toxic Environmental Stresses to Success (ACEs)
Overview of State Requirements Regarding Seclusion and Restraint: Chapter 103

You can find your completion certificates for training or your transcript for credit-bearing courses (license renewal or graduate credit courses) on the homepage of the training system.

When you log in, click the house icon in the top left corner. Scroll down slightly until you see Your certificate history. From there, a certificate button will show next to training and non-credit-bearing courses. A transcript button will appear for any credit-bearing course.

You can find the transcript for a credit-bearing course taken through AEA Learning Online by sigining into your account and navigating to the homepage. From there scroll down to your training history and find the course you are looking for a transcript for. Click the transcript button to the right of the course title and a new page will open with the transcript for you to download, save, and print.

You can also find transcripts on the escWorks site by going to aealearningonline.escworks.com/, signing into your account, and then clicking on the transcript button on the left menu bar.

You can find self-paced courses that are eligible for License Renewal credit on our training website. When you log into training.aealearningonline.org, click the ‘catalog’ button in the top left corner. On the left side, you will see ‘tags’. Select ‘self-paced course’ under tags. Anything in this list that has ‘LR’ or ‘License Renewal Credit’ in the title is eligible for License Renewal Credit.

You can find facilitated courses (with specific start and end dates) eligible for License Renewal credit by visiting aealearningonline.escworks.com. After logging in, click on ‘Calendar of Sessions’ on the left menu bar.

You can find receipts for training and course purchases through AEA Learning Online in your profile. After logging into your account, navigate to your profile and then find the 'transactions' tab. Locate the receipt you want and click the receipt button. It will then open up in a new window where you can download, save, and print your receipt.

To get started getting your Substitute Authorization, it is best to check your qualifications against the requirements on the BoEE website. They state: “Associate's degree or 60 semester hours or higher and the completion of an approved substitute authorization course.” They also have a helpful sheet with resources on checking your higher education institution’s accreditation even if you have your semester hours from an out-of-country institution.

You will also need to create an account with AEA Learning Online. Here are the instructions to complete this step. How to Create an Account.

Finally, you will need to register and take the Iowa Substitute Teaching Authorization Course (Initial Authorization). The cost of the course is $150. Keep in mind you will also have fees for your application through the BoEE and fees for your background check and fingerprinting. All of that is discussed within the course but these additional fees are not paid to AEA Learning Online and are required to complete your application.
